Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle

After watching the massive fire at Demon Slayer headquarters, all the Hashiras rush through the ruins. They see Muzan Kibutsuji. They try to attack him, but suddenly they are teleported to Muzan's Infinity Castle. The movie is about the intense battle between the Demon Slayers and Muzan in his Infinity Castle. It is full of action, drama, and breathtaking animation—a must-watch for all anime lovers!

One Piece: Time Watch

The movie follows the Straw Hat Pirates on a brand-new adventure filled with action, emotion, and powerful battles. The animation is top-notch, making it a must-watch for all anime lovers. At its heart, the story is about a boy named Luffy who dreams of becoming the Pirate King by finding the legendary treasure, One Piece. Along the way, he gathers a loyal crew and faces mighty enemies—all while chasing freedom and dreams.
